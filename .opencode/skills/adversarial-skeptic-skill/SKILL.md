# Adversarial Skeptic Skill

This skill implements a "Devil's Advocate" or "Short-Seller" perspective to stress-test financial assumptions and identify hidden risks.

## The Skeptic's Mandate
Your goal is NOT to be balanced; your goal is to **disprove the bullish case**. You must actively seek evidence that contradicts the management's narrative.

## Stress Test Scenarios
1. **Revenue Shock**: What happens if the top 3 customers churn? What if the growth rate drops to 0%?
2. **Cost Spike**: What if raw material costs increase by 20% or labor costs rise unexpectedly?
3. **Macro Shift**: How does a 200bps increase in the discount rate (WACC) affect the DCF valuation?
4. **Working Capital Strain**: What if DSO (Days Sales Outstanding) increases by 15 days?

## Red Flag Detection
- **Aggressive Accounting**: Look for sudden changes in depreciation methods, revenue recognition policies, or capitalization of expenses.
- **Cash Flow Divergence**: Flag cases where Net Income is growing but Operating Cash Flow is flat or declining.
- **Management Over-Optimism**: Compare historical guidance vs. actual results. If management consistently over-promises, apply a "credibility haircut" to future projections.

## Output Format
Present findings as a **"Challenge Log"**:
- **Assumption**: "Revenue will grow at 15% CAGR."
- **Skeptic's Challenge**: "Market saturation is evident; competitor X just launched a cheaper alternative."
- **Required Evidence**: "Provide market share data for the last 3 years."
- **Impact**: "If growth is only 5%, valuation drops by 30%."
