# Peer Comparison Skill

This skill provides the methodology for relative valuation and benchmarking against industry competitors.

## Peer Set Selection
1. **Quantitative Filters**: Select peers based on:
   - Revenue size (within 0.5x to 2x of target).
   - Profitability margins (similar EBITDA margin range).
   - Market Cap (comparable scale).
2. **Qualitative Filters**: Match by business model, geography, customer base, and product overlap.
3. **Outlier Removal**: Use the Interquartile Range (IQR) method to remove extreme outliers from the peer group to avoid skewing the median.

## Benchmarking Metrics
- **Valuation Multiples**: P/E, EV/EBITDA, P/S, P/BV.
- **Operational Ratios**: Revenue Growth, Gross Margin, ROE, ROIC, Debt/Equity.
- **Efficiency Ratios**: Asset Turnover, Inventory Turnover, DSO.

## Analysis Workflow
- **Relative Positioning**: Plot the target company against the peer median on a scatter plot (e.g., Growth vs. Multiple).
- **Premium/Discount Justification**: If the target trades at a premium to peers, identify the specific driver (e.g., higher growth, stronger brand, better margins).
- **Implied Valuation**: Calculate the implied share price based on the peer median multiple.
